General Consultation
Patient: Harper Lewis, Age: 31
Date: 03/19/2025

Harper Lewis, a 31-year-old female with no significant prior conditions, presented with complaints of intermittent abdominal cramping, diarrhea, and mild nausea over the past three days. Her abdominal examination revealed diffuse tenderness without guarding or rebound tenderness. Differential diagnoses included viral gastroenteritis versus an early inflammatory bowel process. The management plan emphasized hydration, dietary modifications (clear liquids transitioning to a BRAT diet), and symptomatic treatment with antispasmodic medications. Harper was advised to monitor her symptoms closely and return if there was any sign of dehydration or worsening pain.
