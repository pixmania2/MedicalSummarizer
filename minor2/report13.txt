General Consultation
Patient: Isabella Evans, Age: 41
Date: 03/11/2025

Isabella Evans, a 41-year-old female with a history of migraine headaches, visited the clinic with a complaint of an unusually severe headache accompanied by nausea and blurred vision over the past 48 hours. The physical and neurological examinations were normal except for the reported symptoms. The differential diagnosis considered a migraine variant versus a possible sinus infection. The treatment involved a combination of migraine-specific medications, including a triptan, and supportive care with hydration and rest. Isabella was advised to maintain a headache diary and return if her symptoms escalated or if she developed any neurological deficits.
