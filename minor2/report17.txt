Lab Report
Patient: Charlotte Reed, Age: 68
Date: 03/15/2025

Charlotte Reed, a 68-year-old female with a long-standing history of osteoporosis, underwent laboratory testing as part of her routine evaluation. The complete blood count was within normal limits, while her CMP revealed stable kidney and liver function. Bone turnover markers were slightly elevated, prompting a review of her current calcium and vitamin D supplementation regimen. Additionally, her lipid profile was monitored and found to be within acceptable ranges. The physician recommended adjusting her osteoporosis medications and ensuring optimal nutritional intake, along with scheduling a dual-energy X-ray absorptiometry (DEXA) scan for further evaluation.
