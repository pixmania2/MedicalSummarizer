General Consultation
Patient: Mia Thompson, Age: 27
Date: 03/13/2025

Mia Thompson, a 27-year-old female with a history of mild anxiety, came to the clinic with complaints of palpitations, intermittent shortness of breath, and a racing heart over the last three days. Her vital signs were mostly within normal limits except for a slightly elevated heart rate. A thorough cardiac examination, including an ECG, showed no acute abnormalities. The clinical impression suggested a possible anxiety-induced response or a benign arrhythmia. Management included reassurance, lifestyle modifications, and a short course of low-dose beta-blockers. Mia was advised to avoid stimulants such as caffeine and to follow up in a week if symptoms persisted.
