General Consultation
Patient: Liam Nguyen, Age: 37
Date: 03/06/2025

Liam Nguyen, a 37-year-old male with a prior history of seasonal allergies, presented with complaints of a non-productive cough, mild chest tightness, and intermittent sore throat over the past four days. Physical examination revealed clear lung sounds with subtle expiratory wheezes at the bases. Considering his allergy background and current symptoms, the differential included viral bronchitis versus allergic bronchospasm. The management strategy included a trial of inhaled bronchodilators and a course of a mild antibiotic to address any potential bacterial component. Liam was advised to monitor his symptoms and return for a follow-up if his breathing difficulties worsened.
