ab Report
Patient: Lucas Ford, Age: 65
Date: 03/12/2025

Lucas Ford, a 65-year-old male with a history of mild chronic kidney disease, presented for a follow-up laboratory evaluation. His CBC showed a mild anemia (hemoglobin 11.2 g/dL), and the CMP revealed a serum creatinine of 1.6 mg/dL, slightly higher than his baseline. Electrolytes were within acceptable limits, although his potassium level was at the upper end. The lipid panel was unremarkable. The physician discussed the need for further monitoring of his renal function and advised possible adjustments in his medications to prevent further kidney impairment, along with a recommendation for a renal diet.
