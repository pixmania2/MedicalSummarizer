General Consultation
Patient: James Kim, Age: 52
Date: 03/18/2025

James Kim, a 52-year-old male with a past medical history of mild arthritis, presented with complaints of joint stiffness and moderate pain in his hands and knees, which had worsened over the past week. On examination, there was palpable tenderness and limited range of motion in the affected joints, but without significant swelling. The working diagnosis included osteoarthritis flare versus early rheumatoid arthritis, prompting ordering of inflammatory markers and rheumatoid factor testing. James was prescribed non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs (NSAIDs) for pain relief and referred to physical therapy. A follow-up appointment was arranged in two weeks to review lab results and assess clinical progress.
