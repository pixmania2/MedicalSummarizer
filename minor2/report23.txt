General Consultation
Patient: Sofia Delgado, Age: 36
Date: 03/21/2025

Sofia Delgado, a 36-year-old female with a history of mild eczema, presented with complaints of a pruritic rash, mild fever, and joint discomfort that had developed over the last three days. The rash was noted on her arms and torso, with slight scaling and redness. Differential considerations included an allergic reaction versus a viral exanthem. A skin scraping was performed to rule out a fungal infection, and initial laboratory work including CBC and CRP was ordered. The treatment plan consisted of a short course of oral antihistamines, topical corticosteroids, and advice to avoid potential allergens. A follow-up was arranged for one week to review her progress.
