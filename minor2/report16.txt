General Consultation
Patient: Ethan Rivera, Age: 39
Date: 03/14/2025

Ethan Rivera, a 39-year-old male with a history of mild seasonal allergies, presented with complaints of nasal congestion, sinus pressure, and occasional sneezing for the past four days. Physical examination confirmed inflamed nasal mucosa and slight tenderness over the sinus regions. The differential diagnosis favored allergic rhinitis with a possible viral superinfection. Treatment recommendations included the use of intranasal corticosteroids, oral antihistamines, and increased fluid intake. He was also provided with instructions to monitor his symptoms and return for a reassessment in five days if no improvement occurred.
