 General Consultation
Patient: Grace Miller, Age: 38
Date: 03/23/2025

Grace Miller, a 38-year-old female with a background of mild seasonal allergies, sought consultation due to persistent sinus pressure, headache, and a low-grade fever over the past five days. Physical examination revealed tenderness over the maxillary sinuses and nasal congestion. The differential diagnosis included acute sinusitis versus a viral upper respiratory tract infection. The treatment plan involved initiating a course of amoxicillin-clavulanate to cover possible bacterial sinusitis, along with decongestants and saline nasal rinses. Grace was advised to monitor her temperature and symptoms, with a follow-up visit scheduled in one week to evaluate her response to treatment.
