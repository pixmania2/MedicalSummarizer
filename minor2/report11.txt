General Consultation
Patient: Ava Sullivan, Age: 33
Date: 03/09/2025

Ava Sullivan, a 33-year-old female with no significant previous illnesses, presented with complaints of generalized fatigue, mild joint aches, and low-grade fever lasting for five days. On examination, her vital signs were stable, and joint examination revealed no significant swelling or redness. The clinical suspicion ranged from a mild viral syndrome to early rheumatologic process. She was advised to increase her fluid intake, rest adequately, and use acetaminophen for fever and pain control. The doctor scheduled a follow-up appointment in a week and recommended basic blood work including inflammatory markers to rule out underlying conditions if symptoms persisted.
